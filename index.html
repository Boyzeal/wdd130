Osun Nigeria

Osun State, in southwest Nigeria, is known as the "State of Living Springs" 🌳. It's home to the sacred Osun River and the Osun Osogbo Sacred Grove, a UNESCO World Heritage Site. The state celebrates its rich culture through festivals like the Osun Osogbo Festival. The state boasts lush forests, rolling hills and the famous Osun River, which is central to the state's identity. With a mix of Urban central like Oshogbo and a rural landscapes, Osun offers a glimpse into Nigeria's diverse cultural tapestry.
